## Two bugs this sweep caught in the prober itself

Recorded because both produced plausible but wrong availability reports. AiKi's discovery tools must not confuse a failure in the measuring instrument with a failure in a provider's service.

**1. Self-inflicted unreachability.** An earlier run reported **32.6% `UNREACHABLE`**. It was ours: 8 concurrent agents × 3 probes at one host (129 of 157 endpoints share it) made it time out, and we recorded our own overload as evidence about their endpoint. Probed serially: **HTTP 200 in 0.87s**.

→ **Per-host serialisation with a 350ms courtesy gap.** Evidence about a third party must never be an artifact of how hard we hit them.

**2. D1 defaulting to LIVE when it could not run.** An earlier run reported **7 `LIVE`**. All 7 were the same URL - `q402.quackai.ai/api/relay/info` → `{"facilitator":"0xfc77FF…"}`, identical for every input. D1 never ran: the URL has no identifier to vary, so only one probe was made, and the classifier fell through to `LIVE`.

→ Two fixes. A **D1-inapplicable guard**: fewer than 2 probes means agent-specificity is *unproven* → `DEGRADED`, never `LIVE`. And **D10**, a new rule:

> If several distinct agents register the identical URL, that endpoint is not agent-specific **by construction** - whatever it returns.

D1 catches *same bytes, different inputs*. D10 catches *same URL, different agents* - which D1 structurally cannot see. **D10 fired 7 times here**, on precisely the cases that were previously false positives.

## What this means for AiKi

- None of the 400 sampled agents was classified `LIVE` by this sweep. That finding supports checking agent services before presenting them as available; it is not a measurement of every hiring path or provider in the market.
- `IMPOSTOR_STATIC` accounted for 33.3% of the sample. The sweep does not establish the current registry-wide share or what every competitor detects.
- The result raised a supply risk and motivated reference-agent work. It does not establish that supply can only be created, nor cover human providers.
- D8 found no reciprocal proof in the 400-agent sample. That observation is scoped to the sample and date.
- The original report describes the measurement as reproducible, rule-attributed and self-correcting, with each verdict carrying its rule. Independent reproduction also needs the missing raw artifact noted above.

AiKi's [current product definition](../../docs/PRODUCT.md) is a marketplace for humans and agents to get work done together. This sweep supports the quality of agent discovery. It does not replace evidence that hiring, delivery, review, payment or human-agent handoffs work.
