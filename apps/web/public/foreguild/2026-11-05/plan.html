Foreguild revenue operating plan

Start with paid SDK and API checks. Let prediction markets choose business decisions within strict limits.

Foreguild's first buyer is a crypto developer-tool maintainer. An agent team replays a quickstart, checks agreed behaviours, and returns evidence the buyer can reproduce. Each release creates a reason to order again. Independent evaluation checks the work before crypto payment.

AiKi already implements tasks, assignment, delivery, review, budgets, fee accounting and refunds. Foreguild would add stablecoin payments, service operations and binding futarchy. Page 11 separates existing code from proposed additions. [1, 2]

100 USDCProposed price per accepted pack
20 USDCBase contribution per pack
75 packsBase first 90 operating days

Planning assumptions. These prices, margins and customer targets are unproven.

How it works

Start with qualified providers and a fixed acceptance template. Workers earn for meeting the contract, including documenting defects in the buyer's software. Agents handle intake, routing, testing, evaluation and accounting. Humans handle initial sales, exceptions and emergencies.

Maximize earned fees within fixed budgets, positive contribution and service standards. Once deployment and market checks pass, the executor must follow the market's eligible choice. The team cannot override it with a business vote.

Pilot targets

  • Customers: ten independent paying teams, five repeat buyers and 40 accepted packs in month three.
  • Economics: positive contribution after failed work and support costs; a credible path to 137 accepted packs per month.
  • Autonomy: one small binding decision, if liquidity and execution checks pass. Otherwise, disclose the limitation and retain named human operators.

The proposed funding cap is 15,000 USDC for setup, labor and reserves. Neither Seer nor AiKi has committed it. The base case loses 6,050 USDC in operations, plus a 6,000-USDC setup and review allowance. Release funds in stages. Stop when the evidence fails the next gate.

02 · Work and agent responsibilities

Run a complete work cycle

Scope→Fund→Assign→Execute→Verify→Pay

Example brief

“Replay version 1.4's Node quickstart in a clean environment. Check installation, test-credential authentication, a successful request, an invalid-input response and the retry example. Return commands, pinned dependencies, outputs and a reproduction for each discrepancy. Use the sandbox only.”

This example is not a completed AiKi job. Before funding, the buyer approves the cases and limits. Store immutable hashes of the brief, quote, deadlines, allowed endpoints and acceptance rule. Agent buyers follow the same flow under their owner's mandate.

Role Work Authority limit
Intake agent Fill the template; find missing access or unclear checks Cannot expand scope or charge without approval
Dispatcher Match coverage, reliability, cost and capacity Uses a qualified shortlist; registration alone is insufficient
Execution agent Run isolated, time-limited tests; sign evidence; list blocked cases No production keys, unrestricted network access or publishing authority
Evaluation agent Reproduce failures, sample passes, check artifacts and versions Separate operator; cannot change the brief
Treasury agent Reconcile escrow, quotes, fees, payouts and reserves Only contract-permitted payments
Growth/reporting agents Research prospects, total costs and publish revenue evidence Human operator controls initial outreach and promises

Assign and assess work

Recruit three execution providers and two independent evaluators. Require the same calibration tests. Match supported runtimes and available capacity; show the buyer the worker and quote. Reliability matters alongside price.

Evaluators check the environment, full case table, runnable reproductions and evidence integrity. A documented product defect can pass. Fabricated runs, missing cases and unexplained version changes require correction or rejection. Accept a blocked dependency only if the brief permits it; otherwise resolve the scope first.

Include one correction cycle. Quote extensions separately. Subcontracting must stay within the original authority and budget. Each child task names its payer. Allow only approved task templates and enforce the parent spending cap.

Keep payloads private. Publish hashes and settlement evidence for governance. Publish reports only with buyer permission.

03 · Settlement and remedies

Pay workers in crypto

Propose Base USDC, with ETH for gas. This requires a new integration. AiKi's Points cannot be redeemed. BSC token transfers and Points purchases do not prove worker cash-out. [2]

Money movements

Quotes name three charges: 60 USDC for execution, 15 for evaluation, and 25 for Foreguild coordination. Earned platform fees are the model's revenue metric. Provider pay covers their execution and model costs. Foreguild separately budgets orchestration, support, payouts and losses.

Release 40 to the worker and 10 to the evaluator after initial acceptance. Hold 20/5/25 until the retest passes or its seven-day request window expires. A timely request extends completion by at most 48 hours. Earn the fee only at final closure. Every modelled accepted pack is a finally closed pack. Missed retests follow the remedy process.

Separate customer escrow, earned fees, operating funds and governance collateral. Never lend, invest or trade customer principal, or count it as revenue. Before funding, bind addresses, job IDs, quotes and settlement terms.

Verify an established settlement component before using it. The adapter must handle confirmations, duplicate requests, refunds, token decimals and reconciliation. After a crash, read the transaction receipt before retrying. Real payments require independent review and failure tests.

Payment stages

100 USDCBuyer funds the scoped job
50 USDC40 worker + 10 evaluator after initial acceptance
50 USDC20 worker + 5 evaluator + 25 fee held until closure

Receipts link the task, quote, parties, payment stage and transaction hash. A timely dispute blocks the affected release. Replacing a provider uses the original holdback and any approved remedy reserve.

Reconcile token balances with customer obligations after each settlement. Any mismatch pauses new funding and treasury spending. Report pending and earned fees separately.

04 · Quality and remedies

Close disputes within a defined budget

Independent evaluation protects buyers and workers. Valid evidence earns payment even when the product fails. Agree on review, correction and appeal rules before work starts.

Remedy rules

Trigger Proposed remedy
Unclaimed job or failure before work starts Cancel; return full escrow
Timely valid delivery Buyer has 24 hours after evaluation to accept or identify a contract defect
Silent buyer Release the first payment only with evaluator approval; retain the retest holdback; freeze disputed funds
Invalid or missing work Allow one correction within 24 hours, then an independent appeal
Disputed completion Independent human reviewer applies the original brief within 72 hours; freeze affected escrow
Appeal misses its deadline Refund the buyer; pay documented worker/evaluator effort from the remedy reserve within its cap; suspend orders until resolved

Agree on partial refunds and compensation in advance. Cap pilot remedy liability at 100 USDC per disputed pack, covering defined worker, evaluator and appeal costs. AiKi's current dispute route does not supply this policy. Reserve coverage before accepting jobs. Stop intake when coverage runs short.

Kleros arbitration requires verification of the court, fees, appeals and deployment. Its proposed Agentic Courts were still under vote on the research date. Keep work evaluation, job appeals and the revenue-market oracle separate. [8]

Operating limits

Start with five concurrent packs. Use expiring sandbox credentials, restrict network access and retain evidence. Check for common ownership between workers and evaluators. Humans can stop unsafe work or resolve appeals, but cannot rewrite acceptance terms after delivery.

Name an operator and backup reviewer before taking orders. Record response times, rejection reasons, rework and compensation. Humans review evaluator disagreement and suspected self-dealing. Agents cannot approve their own evidence.

The 1-USDC loss provision is an estimate, not an obligation cap. If losses rise, revise the offer and model. Contingency covers early variation; depleted reserves require an intake pause.

06 · Capital and sensitivity

Fund the pilot in stages

Funding depends on buyer commitments, an implementation quote and a reviewed payment path. Do not assume contest winnings will fund it.

Proposed funding

Use Base allocation in USDC
Setup, implementation and independent review 6,000
Net operating loss, including labor 6,050
Market collateral at risk 500
Remedies and cost overruns 1,000
Unallocated within the 15,000 cap 1,450

The 500 market reserve is capital at risk. It is neither an operating expense nor guaranteed recoverable cash. The model's 200 governance expense covers execution, oracle and market operations. Market-authorized channel spend sits within the acquisition budget, capped at 300. It earns no separate revenue.

Release at most 500 for samples and scoping. Release the remaining setup allowance only against buyer commitments and a credible quote. If the quote exceeds the cap, reduce scope or obtain a revised budget.

Break-even and failure costs

At month-three overhead of 2,725 and contribution of 20 per pack, break-even needs 137 accepted packs/month. That is roughly 35 teams buying four packs. Fifty such teams would produce 1,275/month before setup recovery and tax, if costs stayed constant. Costs may rise with volume.

If 10% of attempts fail at an unrecovered cost of 45, losses equal 5 per accepted pack. Replacing the base loss provision with that amount reduces contribution to 16. Break-even rises to 171 packs. The broader downside assumes variable cost of 12 and needs 210 packs. Measure failure costs before scaling.

07 · Distribution and capital

Reach the first repeat buyers

The founder sells first. Agents research public releases and draft briefs; humans review and send outreach. No prospects, introductions or partnerships are assumed.

Acquisition experiment

Find 60 developer-tool teams with maintained SDK/API docs, recent releases and a budget owner. Produce five sample packs on public or authorized examples. Show clean results as well as defects.

Target 20 buyer conversations and ten paid pilots. These are experiment targets, not conversion benchmarks. Ask about broken quickstarts, review effort, release frequency, buying authority and the 100-USDC price. Get three purchase commitments before the main implementation spend. Take funds only after payment and remedy checks pass.

Offer the next release check after each accepted pack. Buyer-owned triggers or agents may reorder within an agreed cap. Exclude samples, internal work, grants and prizes from paid-customer cohorts. Acquisition cost includes channel spend and founder sales time from the labor allowance. Measure payback against actual contribution.

Funding gates

Stage Evidence required
Prelaunch Five samples, ten budget-owner interviews, three price commitments, agreed funding and integration scope
Days 1–30 Ten accepted paid packs; at least three unrelated buyers; target ≥90% acceptance and buyer review under 15 minutes
Days 31–60 Six paying teams, three repeat buyers; positive contribution after support and failed work
Days 61–90 Ten paying teams, five reorders within 30 days, 40 month-three packs; target payback within three months

If ten qualified interviews yield no willingness to pay, revise the offer. Pause acquisition if the latest ten packs show acceptance below 85% or median human handling above 45 minutes. Pause intake for unpaid remedies or inadequate reserves. Small samples guide spending; they do not prove demand.

08 · Futarchy mechanism

Let markets choose a bounded policy

Founders and treasury stewards set the charter: maximize earned fees while protecting customer funds, limiting spend, maintaining positive expected contribution and meeting service standards. Once binding operation starts, eligible market decisions need no further business vote.

Define revenue before trading

R30 measures platform fees received on eligible jobs finally closed in the 30 days from T0. Subtract their refunds, credits and disputed fees as of day 44. Count unresolved disputes as zero. Calculate in USDC micro-units.

Exclude customer principal, provider/evaluator payouts, Points purchases, donations, token proceeds, treasury transfers, subsidized internal work and disclosed related-party purchases. Verify customer independence through records and disclosures; wallet counts cannot prove it. Record later reversals in the accounts without reopening finalized markets.

The first choice

Compare incumbent acquisition policy A with alternative B for the 100-USDC offer. Establish A during the human-run pilot. Before trading, freeze audiences, creative, destinations, schedules and equal spending caps. Keep pricing, providers and acceptance standards fixed.

Set deadlines before trading. Price observations end at D. The executor can commit until D + 1 hour; service starts at T0 = D + 2 hours. No timely commitment means NO-ACTION. Reject late authorizations. Measure revenue from T0 and adjust it through day 44.

Create a Seer categorical parent for the executor's timely choice: A, B or NO-ACTION, plus the protocol's invalid result. Each policy gets a conditional scalar child measuring R30 from 0 to 5,000 USDC. Seer documents these components. Foreguild still needs a price adapter and executor. [9, 10]

Conditional forecast = 5,000 × price(HIGH) ÷ [price(HIGH) + price(LOW)]

Price both tokens in the same branch collateral. Check valid mass and invalid risk. Raw base-currency prices also reflect selection probability. Compare time-weighted executable quotes.

For the selected valid branch, HIGH pays the scalar share of parent collateral; LOW pays the rest. Values outside the bounds clip. Invalid resolution pays the invalid token. Unchosen branches lose base-collateral value; they receive no automatic refund. Do not invent an unobserved outcome. Explain positions and hedging to traders. [10, 11]

Resolve against evidence

Publish final fee/refund events, job and acceptance hashes, exclusions and calculations. Two independent reporters check them. Bonded Reality.eth answers and the configured arbitration path settle the market; reporter agreement alone does not. Challenges delay redemption. [12]

Conditional forecasts do not prove causation. Use holdouts and stable conditions to improve later evidence. Prohibit concurrent policy changes and show forecast uncertainty.

09 · Governance execution and limits

Make the decision binding within its limits

Build and independently review the executor and price adapter. MetaDAO offers a precedent for time-weighted conditional decisions, but uses a different chain, objective and liquidity. Its parameters do not establish Foreguild's security. [13]

Activation checks

Allow 24 hours for discovery, then observe prices for 24 hours. Require quotes less than five minutes old, executable depth on both valid legs in base-collateral/USDC terms, and configured spread limits. Invalid-token value must stay at or below 1% of the complete child set. B must beat A by the greater of 10% or 25 USDC in forecast revenue. Replay tests must verify adapter rules and complete observation intervals.

Start with 25 USDC of binding spend authority, within a 300-USDC channel-policy ceiling. A 500-USDC market reserve does not prove resistance to manipulation. Test strategies that move the normalized, time-weighted decision across its threshold, including price spikes, liquidity removal and unwinding. Require net unrecoverable attacker cost above five times authorized spend. Volume and temporary collateral do not count. This is a minimum check, not a security proof; outside incentives and collusion remain risks.

If it fails, reduce authority or add independent liquidity. Exclude wash volume and wallet counts. Governance may cost more than the initial 25-USDC decision. Scale only when expected benefits exceed governance costs.

Condition Executor action
All checks pass; B clears the threshold Commit B; unlock its capped authority
All checks pass; B misses the threshold Commit A with the same cap
Missing data, insufficient depth, invalid conditions, expiry or reserve failure Commit NO-ACTION; authorize no added spend
Exploit or reserve loss Security council may pause with a recorded reason; it cannot choose another policy

Any keeper may finalize. The executor records the policy hash and allowance. Agents receive approved destinations and asset, daily/total caps, expiry, job/policy IDs and replay protection. They cannot raise budgets, borrow customer funds, change the offer or upgrade contracts.

Three stages

  1. Observed operation: named humans run jobs and shadow forecasts. This is not autonomous futarchy.
  2. Binding pilot: after market, payment and failure tests pass, an eligible 25-USDC decision binds agents to the charter.
  3. Expansion: approach the 300 ceiling only with stronger liquidity, evidence and economics. Amendments affect future cycles.

Seer lists Base infrastructure. Before funding, verify the selected factory's bytecode, collateral and arbitrator. Jobs and markets may use different assets; conversions use the governance reserve. The design assumes no automatic cross-chain execution and needs no new governance token. [14]

Reproduce the model

The model includes editable inputs, scenario CSVs and a Python generator. Revenue = accepted packs × 25. Contribution = revenue − platform variable costs. Operating result subtracts labor, infrastructure, acquisition and governance. Setup is separate. Reserve collateral and contingency without counting them again as expenses. Exclude escrow and refund liabilities from spendable treasury.

Even the upside loses money during the pilot. No token gains, investment yield, market volume, unverified users or contest prizes fund the forecast. Validate prices, provider costs, delivery, repeat purchases, payments and governance before scaling.
